# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **MusicApp** application (hereby referred to as "the App"), created by Ram as a Free service. This service is provided "as is" and is intended for use primarily as an offline, local music player.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed to function strictly on your device. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personally identifiable information or personal data.** 

To provide the intended features, the App requires certain device permissions. Note that data accessed through these permissions is used locally and is never uploaded to our servers:

* **Storage & Media Files (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`):** 
  The App requests access to your device's media storage exclusively to discover, organize, and play your local audio files. We do not modify or delete your files without your explicit action.
* **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** 
  The App requires microphone access to enable Voice Search. Audio input is processed locally by your device's built-in speech recognition service (such as Google’s native Android voice recognition) to perform the search. We do not record, store, or transmit your voice data.
* **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** 
  The App uses notifications to provide persistent media playback controls when the app is in the background or when the screen is locked.

## 2. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks that collect user data. Speech recognition for the voice search feature relies on your device's native OS services, which are governed by the privacy policy of your device manufacturer and/or operating system provider (e.g., Google).

## 3. Log Data and Crash Reports

We do not collect or monitor crash logs or usage metrics on external servers. Any error logs generated during the App’s operation are kept locally on your device. Android may independently gather anonymous crash reporting if you have opted into Google's usage and diagnostics sharing.

## 4. Security

Because the App offline nature means it does not transmit your personal data or files securely over the internet, your information is naturally protected within the sandboxed environment of your Android device. We are dedicated to using the requested permissions strictly for the App’s core functionality.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes. Any modifications will be effective immediately once updated on this page.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out.
